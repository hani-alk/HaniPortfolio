Record holder

Jordanian national record on the 2×2 single — 1.80 seconds.

I hold the Jordanian national record for the 2×2 single at 1.80 seconds. At Jordan Nationals 2022 I took silver in 2×2 and bronze in 3×3.

I average around 10 seconds on the 3×3, and once held the second national record on 3×3 blindfolded.

While at King's Academy I also won a nation-wide inter-school chess tournament.
