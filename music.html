Piano

Toward the end of my first 16 years of ABRSM Piano, Reading, and Theory training, I fell in love with composition, arrangement, improvisation, and production — breaking the rules. So I continued on my own.

Production

I started learning electronic music production in 7th grade and quickly became obsessed with the sound of EDM, before discovering all the genres and forms of experimentation I love today.
